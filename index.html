How It Works

Platora chooses the right monitoring path for each plate.

Add a vehicle once. Platora checks permitted sources where available and routes restricted states into an authorized compliance-mail workflow.

01

Add plate

Create an account with email, phone, plate number, and state.

02

Determine coverage

Tier 1 uses permitted lookup. Tier 2 uses authorized vehicle compliance mail.

03

Organize activity

Tickets, tolls, notices, due dates, documents, and payment status land in one place.

04

Pay with proof

Pay manually, enable AutoPay rules, and keep receipts in your dashboard.

AutoPay

User-controlled payment rules.

AutoPay is off by default. When enabled, Platora only pays verified items that match the saved card, consent, limits, and rules selected by the account owner.

Auto-pay tolls under $100 Require approval over $500 Never auto-pay disputed items Review unfamiliar authorities
